## The Beauty of Complex Analysis

### The Triumph of a "Nonexistent" Number

$i$ began as "subtle and useless." But it was precisely this nonexistent number that gave every polynomial a root, every rotation an algebraic expression, and unified exponential with trigonometric functions. **Mathematics' tolerance — allowing the "impossible" to exist — opened an entire new world.**

### Euler's Formula: The Signature of the Universe

$$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$

Five of the most important constants in one equation. No long derivations, no complicated conditions — only pure connection. It is repeatedly voted the most beautiful mathematical formula, because it contains the deepest unification in the most compact form.

### The Miracle of Analytic Functions

The requirement of complex differentiability is so strict that analytic functions are "forced" to possess infinitely many good properties: once differentiable at a point, differentiable everywhere; satisfying the mean value property; completely determined by boundary values. **The harsher the conditions, the more astonishing the structure** — analytic functions embody this aesthetic.

### Conformal Maps: Guardians of Angle

Conformal maps preserve angles. Straightening a complex region into a disk, deforming an airfoil into a cylinder — computation becomes simple, while angles stay faithful. **Guarding the least changeable quantity through deformation** — an elegance unique to complex analysis.

### From Real Integrals to the Complex Plane

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{dx}{1+x^2} = \pi$$

A seemingly unsolvable real integral, obtained effortlessly through contour integration on the complex plane. **Change the dimension and the hard problem vanishes** — complex analysis teaches not just mathematics, but a way of seeing.

> The beauty of complex analysis: a number once scorned as "useless" ultimately unified algebra, geometry, analysis, and physics.
