## The Beauty of Bayes' Theorem

### Self-Correction

$$P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A)\,P(A)}{P(B)}$$

The beauty of Bayes' theorem: it teaches us to **update beliefs with new evidence**. Prior + evidence = posterior. This is no mere formula but an epistemology — science itself is the ongoing process of revising beliefs with evidence.

### Counterintuitive Wisdom

- **The testing paradox**: a 98%-accurate test leaves only a 50% chance of disease after a positive result
- **The triumph of the prior**: the same formula, different priors, dramatically different conclusions
- **Continuous learning**: today's posterior is tomorrow's prior

These results remind us: **don't trust intuition — trust the calculation.**

### From Gambling to AI

A formula from 1763 runs every day inside spam filters, self-driving cars, and medical diagnosis. **An idea spanning two centuries is still the core of intelligence.**

> The beauty of Bayes lies in this: it wrote "learning" — humanity's most natural ability — as a single elegant, concise formula.
